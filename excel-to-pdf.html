📊 Excel to PDF

Choose an Excel file (.xlsx, .xls or .csv) and turn it into a PDF. Your file is never uploaded.

The PDF follows the print settings saved in your Excel file (print area, paper size, orientation, margins, fit to page). Text stays sharp, and colors, bold text, borders and merged cells are kept. Hidden rows, columns and sheets are skipped, and empty pages are not created. Old .xls and .csv files have no color information, so they get a light grid.
