📽️ PowerPoint to PDF

Choose a PowerPoint (.pptx) file and turn it into a PDF. Your file is never uploaded.

Each slide becomes one PDF page with the same size and layout. Slides are drawn from the file in your browser, so fonts that are not installed on this device are replaced and some effects (animations, videos, special charts) may look different. Old .ppt files: open in PowerPoint and save as .pptx first.
